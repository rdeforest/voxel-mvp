# Charla Sessions

*Charla* (Spanish: a chat, a talk). The standing routine for clarification
sessions. Recorded 2026-09-30.

## Purpose

Reduce how much Robert slows Claude down by not having said what he wants.
Each session works on whatever most needs correction or clarification, so
that the next session (or a Claude Code run) can decide more on its own.

## How one starts

On a day Robert has the energy, he opens a conversation with:

> time for another charla session

## What Claude does

1. **Check in** to ClodForest as `voxel-mvp` and report the inbox (one line
   per message; act on nothing without Robert's OK).
2. **Read the state:** [`00_INDEX.md`](00_INDEX.md) (current priority),
   [`../../reference/12-known-contradictions.md`](../../reference/12-known-contradictions.md),
   and the latest round in [`demo-role-play.md`](demo-role-play.md).
3. **Propose the one thing that most needs clarifying,** favoring the
   current priority, and say in a sentence why it's the biggest blocker.
   Robert can take it or pick something else.
4. **Work it one question at a time.** Short replies. Use whichever method
   fits: a story card, a role-play round, resolving a contradiction, or a
   direct question.
5. **Keep a list of the questions Claude had to ask.** Each one is something
   the docs couldn't answer; the list is the backlog for the next session.
6. **Before ending, land the results in the repo,** not only in ClodForest:
   decisions into the docs that own them, resolved items removed from the
   contradiction register, new open questions added to it, card candidates
   handed off for filing. Report what changed.

## Units

A **project day** is about six hours of Robert and Claude working together,
not a calendar day. A charla session may be part of a day; priorities like
"top priority for two days" are counted in project days.
