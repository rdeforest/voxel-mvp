# Demo Role-Play

A way of discovering what the game is by playing it out loud before it
exists. Recorded 2026-09-30 from the 2026-09-27/28 chat sessions.

## Format

- Robert describes the game's opening and says what happens.
- Claude plays the player: says what it notices and what it wants to try.
- Run for an hour or two. Collect every feature that comes up.
- Afterward, trim the collection to a set small enough to judge for
  viability against the Steam Fest demo.
- Pick the demo's physics **hero moment** (the trailer clip). It decides
  which solver pieces must exist by the Fest.
- Jamin and Cecilia will want the result.

Anything that comes up as a sentence a player would tell a friend is also a
story-card candidate; note it for the deck.

## Round 1 (2026-09-28): the opening

- Fade from white to a natural tropical beach: washed-up seaweed and shells,
  gull tracks, crabs, ocean on one side and dense trees on the other, a
  clearly inactive volcano looming.
- Sand behaves by moisture: dry and powdery, damp and stiff, wet and sludgy.
  Holes collapse when undermined; digging reaches the water table; waves run
  up dug channels and erode them; the beach drifts back to equilibrium.
  (Reference liked: the *Sandcastle* demo's water.)
- Tool-making comes back as memories (twine from plant fibers).

Scope decisions that came out of it (2026-09-28):

- Wildlife is a function of habitat (grass → insects → birds → cats;
  roots/mushrooms → boar → canids), not a coupled predator/prey simulation.
  A full dynamic ecosystem belongs to the ultimate vision, not this game.
- When habitat disappears, creatures stop appearing. Existing ones carry on
  until out of sight and hearing, or vanish while the player sleeps. Never
  vanish in view.
- Creatures react to the world but don't reshape it. Footprints and nibbled
  plants are fine; generated dams, burrows or nests are out unless the
  landscape already provides them. No beavers.
- No sex in the game, even between animals; birth is off-camera; creatures
  differ mainly in locomotion and habitat.

Proposed, **not decided**: that the game is centered on materials (honest
stuff the player shapes), with ecology kept low-fidelity. Open; see
[`../../reference/12-known-contradictions.md`](../../reference/12-known-contradictions.md).
